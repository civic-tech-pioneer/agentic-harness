## Goal

The console stays fast on a long session. Only the rows near the viewport are in the DOM (the
transcript is virtualized), the page renders at most once per animation frame however fast the
stream is, and a large-transcript fixture in `npm test` holds the console to limits for input,
Stop, scroll and stream delay. `npm run dev` can open the long session, so a person (the
user-sim) can try it. Everything the console showed before still shows the same way: the end on
open, following the end within 40 px, the "New activity below" pill rule, Needs you scrolling to
the first open ask and focusing Allow once, folded rows keeping their state.

## Acceptance

AC1. Virtualized. With the long fixture (AC6) opened, the transcript holds at most 120 row
     elements (`.wa-row`) in the DOM at any time, while scrolling from the end to the top and
     back, and while the stream runs. The scroll height stands for all rows (estimated heights
     for rows never measured, measured heights once seen), so the scrollbar moves smoothly and
     the reader can reach the first row.
AC2. Kept mounted. A row that holds focus, or a row with an open ask or check, is never removed
     from the DOM while that holds (so a typed deny reason, the focused Allow once and its ask bar
     survive a scroll away and back). A folded row's open or closed state (thought, Explored, a
     card's Disclosure) survives its row leaving the window and coming back: the state is kept
     per row key outside the row component.
AC3. Place (18.6) unchanged: on open the end shows; while within 40 px of the end, new output
     follows; otherwise the view does not move and the pill rule is as before. Row height changes
     above the viewport (a measured row replacing its estimate) do not move what the reader looks
     at by more than 1 row.
AC4. Needs you (18.6) unchanged in effect: with the first open ask's card outside the window, a
     click on Needs you brings the card into the window, scrolls it as today (a card that fits:
     its top; a taller one: its ask bar at the bottom of the view), and puts focus on its Allow
     once with no extra scroll.
AC5. One render per frame. `openSession`'s view updates are coalesced: N kernel messages that
     arrive within one animation frame give at most one `onView` call in that frame (test with a
     controlled `requestAnimationFrame`: 200 deltas between two frames -> 1 render, with the
     view equal to reducing all 200). The reducer still sees every message in order; nothing is
     dropped. With no `requestAnimationFrame` (a node test), updates fall back to a 16 ms timer.
     Closing the session cancels a pending frame.
AC6. The long fixture. `console/fake/long.ts` exports `longStory(n: number)` that yields a
     kernel script of `n` finished entries (users, agents with thoughts, Explored reads, edit
     cards with diffs, program output, decisions, one decided ask) and, after them, a running turn
     with a fast agent-delta stream and one open ask near the start of the window's history (not
     at the end). `npm run dev` with `?long=5000` in the URL opens it; without the parameter the
     page is the story it is today.
AC7. Limits, in `npm test` (jsdom), on the 5 000-entry fixture with the stream playing at 50
     deltas/s, each measured 20 times, the 95th percentile asserted:
     - input: a keystroke in the composer to its text shown: at most 50 ms;
     - Stop: pressing Stop (and Esc in the composer) to the kernel's `interrupt` called and
       "Stopping…" shown: at most 100 ms;
     - scroll: a scroll to a far offset to the rows of that offset in the DOM: at most 100 ms;
     - stream: a delta given to the fake to its text in the DOM: at most 100 ms (17, item delay).
     Opening the 5 000-entry session (attach, paging, first paint of the end) takes at most 2 s.
     The test prints the measured p95s. Its whole file runs in at most 10 s, and the default run
     stays under 2 minutes (README "Test pyramid").
AC8. One slow script, `console/test/long_session.slow.mjs` (S7-1's `slow-kit.mjs`), in Chromium on
     the built `ui/` with `?long=5000`: the end shows on open; at most 120 `.wa-row` elements;
     scrolling to the top shows the first row; Needs you focuses Allow once with its card's ask
     bar inside the transcript area (as `regress_S9_needs_you_focus_visible.slow.mjs` measures);
     no page error. Its header names what jsdom cannot show (real layout and heights).

## Goal

The stage B budgets (TECHNICAL 17, row B) are measured end to end and recorded in 16, "Budget record":
8 fake `claude` sessions on watane's host, 4 for each of 2 owners (the console's `page` and a test
client mod), each 50 deltas/s and 5 finished entries/s of 4 KiB (dev's fake `load` step with `rate`);
item delay source to page, `interrupt` to `idle {interrupted}` at the page; and the state bytes per
finished entry at transcripts of 10 and 10 000 entries. A slow script `npm run test:load` in `console/`,
by hand like `test:host` (exit 0 within budget, 1 over, 2 not run).

## Acceptance

AC1. A second owner: a test client mod `console/test/host/loadclient/` (identity `local/loadclient`)
     whose page calls its own `start`, `attach`-free `interrupt` and `cancel`, which call the kernel's
     `sessions` as itself. Its `access/local/loadclient` = `["scratch"]` is seeded into the stopped
     preview's kernel state by a test-only helper (no kernel call writes `access/`, 9.1); the helper is
     named a test-only reach into the host's state layout in its own header.
AC2. 8 sessions run at once (the page's 4 attached in Chromium pages, the client's 4 unattached), each
     on the fake's `load` with `rate {deltas: 50, entries: 5, bytes: 4096, seconds: 10}`; no start is
     `busy`.
AC3. Item delay source to page: the fake stamps each line with its write time; the page records each
     update's arrival; p95 over the page's sessions ≤ 100 ms (the clock offset is nil: one machine).
AC4. `interrupt` to `idle {interrupted}` at the page: p95 over the page's 4 sessions ≤ 1 s.
AC5. State bytes per finished entry: in the kernel crate (`cargo test` slow-marked or `--ignored`), the
     state bytes read and written per finished entry at transcripts of 10 and 10 000 entries, each ≤ 2 MiB
     and within 10% of each other (case 55's measure at the load's entry size).
AC6. The numbers, the date, the machine (`uname -a`, `nproc`, load average) go in TECHNICAL 16 "Budget
     record", stage B, with the writer busy share cited from watane S100's host record (host 15).

## Goal

Every line a reader gets from the transcript store (`read` and `replay`) carries the `i` of its
place, readable or not. Today `render` in `kernel/src/transcript.rs` gives a readable stored line
back as it is, with whatever `i` it claims; only `unreadable` lines take their position. After
this task a readable line that claims another `i` (or none, or a non-number) is given with
`"i": <its place>`; its other fields are unchanged. So a corrupt or foreign writer of
`entries/<id>/<n>` can never make an entry take another entry's index (9.4: "An `i` the stored
line claims is ignored").

## Acceptance

AC1. A readable stored line (`v` ≤ 1, known kind) at place k that claims `"i": 7`, `"i": 99999`,
     `"i": "x"` or no `i` reads via `read` and `replay` with `i` = k; `v`, `kind`, `at`, `uuid`,
     `body` (and any other field) parse to the same JSON values as stored.
AC2. A readable line whose claimed `i` already equals k is given byte for byte as stored (the
     common path keeps no extra cost: no re-serialization).
AC3. Paging forward and backward (`max` 1, 3, 256) over a chunk with such lines at positions 1,
     the middle and the last of the chunk yields each index 1..=n exactly once in order (extend
     `ac1_unreadable_keeps_its_position_while_paging` or add a sibling test), and `replay(after)`
     from such a place starts at place after + 1.
AC4. A `part` line that claims a wrong `i` also reads with the `i` of its place, and the joiner
     (`Replay`/`push`, or whatever reassembles parts) still joins a group whose parts were stored
     with correct `i` byte for byte (existing part tests pass).
AC5. `unreadable` lines are as S12 built them (S12 tests unchanged).

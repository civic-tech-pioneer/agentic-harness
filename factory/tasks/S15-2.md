## Goal

After this task an entry over 256 KiB, which the kernel stores and sends as consecutive `part`
entries (9.4), shows in Alice's console as the one entry it is: a long agent message reads whole,
whether its parts came live, in the replay after `attach`, across two `read` pages, or across a
page and the live stream. A group the console cannot join (a gap, a mismatch, text that is not one
entry) is one "An event this console does not know" row, never a crash and never a stuck loop.
The scripted fake stores, reads and replays parts as the kernel does.

## Acceptance

AC1. A new pure joiner in `console/kit/join.ts` takes stream messages in order and returns what to
     pass on: a message that is not a `part` entry passes through unchanged (same string); parts
     1..m-1 of a group pass on nothing; part m passes on one bare entry JSON string, the parse of
     the joined `text` of parts 1..m. A `part` entry is either a bare entry object
     (`{"v":1,"i":k,"kind":"part","body":{"n","m","text"}}`) or one inside an `entry` item
     (`_meta.watane.type: "entry"`).
AC2. A group fails when: a part's `n` is not 1 + the previous `n` of the open group, or its `m`
     differs, or its `i` is not 1 + the previous part's `i`; `n`/`m` not positive integers or
     `n > m`; the joined text does not parse to one JSON object; the joined entry's `i` is not the
     last part's `i`; or `m` over 4096 or the joined text over 64 MiB (memory bound; a decision of
     this slice). A failed group passes on one bare `{"v":1,"i":<last part's i seen>,"kind":"unknown","body":{"raw":<the parts' bodies>}}`-shaped
     line that the reducer shows as an `unknown` row at that `i`, and the joiner starts clean. A
     non-part entry arriving while a group is open fails that group (at its last part's `i`)
     before passing the entry on.
AC3. `openSession` (`console/kit/kernel.ts`) runs every message through one joiner before
     `safeReduce`: live messages, replay (before `synced`), and paged entries. Replay and the live
     stream share the joiner of their `attach`; a new `attach` starts a clean joiner.
AC4. Paging (`pageIn`): a group split across two `read` pages joins. A page whose first entry is a
     `part` with `n > 1` (the range began inside a group) reads again from `after = i - n` so the
     group's start is read (9.4: "A client that lost a group's start reads again with
     `after = i - n`"); the read count stays bounded (the governor and the existing "a page that
     adds no index inside its range fails" rule still hold). The newest range shown at once may
     leave out a group whose start is in an older range; the final reduce shows it whole.
AC5. `fetchEntry(n)` (the `~p<n>` refetch) for an entry stored as parts: when `read(n-1, 1)`
     returns a part with `n_part > 1`, it reads `after = n - n_part`, `max = n_part` and joins.
AC6. Fake (`console/fake/kernel.ts`): `preload` and recorded entries may be `part` entries; `read`
     returns them as stored; `attach(after)` whose `after` falls inside a group (the entry after
     `after` is a part with `n > 1`) starts its replay at that group's first part (9.4); a helper
     `partsOf(entry: object, size: number): string[]` (exported from `console/fake/kernel.ts`)
     cuts an entry's serialization into part entries at UTF-8 character boundaries, each `text`
     at most `size` bytes, the parts taking indexes `i-m+1 .. i`.
AC7. Tests (kit level, with the fake): a 300 KiB agent message as 2 parts live; as parts in the
     replay; split over a page boundary of `pageIn`; a page starting mid-group; a multi-byte
     character on a cut; each failure of AC2 gives exactly one `unknown` row and later entries
     still show; reload (attach again after the last index) doubles nothing. `npm test` and
     `npm run typecheck` pass; `ui/` rebuilt and committed; `test/build.test.ts` passes.
AC8. The adversary's held cases of S4 and S9 stay green unchanged: `console/kit/adv_S4.test.tsx`
     (read and attach pacing, no read loop), `console/kit/adv_S9*.test.tsx`.

## Goal

A part group whose joined whole is itself of kind `part` reads as `unreadable`: `Joiner::push`,
when a group completes and its last part has a numeric `i` (k), returns
`{"v":…,"i":k,"kind":"unreadable","at"?…,"raw":"<the joined text>"}` (the same form `render`
gives an unreadable stored line) whenever the joined text's `kind` is `part`, instead of a
readable `part` entry. A whole of any other kind reads as today. Reuse `render`'s unreadable
form (one helper, or a parameter on `render` that the joiner passes); do not write the
unreadable format twice. `read` of stored lines does not change: a stored `part` line still
reads as a `part` (it is one).

## Acceptance

AC1. Two parts (`i` 4 and 5, `m` 2) whose joined text is
     `{"v":1,"i":5,"kind":"part","at":1,"body":{"n":1,"m":1,"text":"x"}}` -> `push` of the second
     returns an entry with `"kind":"unreadable"`, `"i":5`, `"raw"` = the joined text, and `"at":1`.
AC2. The same with the joined `part` whole carrying another `i` (e.g. 1) or no `i`: still
     `unreadable` at `i` 5.
AC3. A joined whole of kind `message` (and every whole kind of `append`) reads as today; the
     `ac10_*`, `ac5_part*`, `adv_s14_*` and `regress_s14_*` tests pass unchanged.
AC4. A last part with no numeric `i` yields what it yields today.
AC5. Through `Transcript::read` on a chunk where two stored lines were rewritten as such a group
     (as `adv_s14_part_group_whole_takes_another_index` builds one), the joined entries a reader
     gets have no `kind: part`. `factory/gate.sh` passes.

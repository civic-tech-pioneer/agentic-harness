## Goal

After this task the console reads the ask, turn, verdict and decision JSON exactly as
`design/TECHNICAL.md` 2.3, 9.3 and 9.4 "The wire" write them (S10), and the scripted fake speaks
that wire too. Alice answers an ask and the console sends the ask entry's `id`, never the tool call
id. The ask card is drawn from the ask entry only: what the stream said about the same call before
or after the ask does not change the header, the diff or the input she sees, while the ask is open
and after its decision; an ask entry without `toolCall` shows no streamed header or diff (S9
adversary RISK a). A `check` shows as a pending check with no answer buttons and never counts in
"Needs you". The status line no longer shows a huge clock when the story is played only to a
running step. Outcomes of sketch S15 (`factory/PLAN.md`) except `part` entries (S15-2) and the
user-sim's layout notes (S15-3).

## Acceptance

AC1. Ask body (9.4): an `ask` entry whose body has a string `id`, `kind` `"ask"` or `"check"`, a
     string `toolCallId` and a number `expires-at` is an ask; one missing any of these (or with
     `expiresAt` instead of `expires-at`, or another `kind`) is an `unknown` row at its `i`, never
     an ask. `toolCall`, `input` and `reach` are read as today; `reach` is ignored on a check.
AC2. Turn (9.3): `waiting` reads `asks` (ask ids) and `expires-at`; a `waiting` with `expiresAt`
     and no `expires-at` is unreadable. `paused` with no `until` is accepted and the status line
     reads "Paused" (no time, two-bars shape, "Resume now" as today); with `until`, "Paused until
     hh:mm" as today. Both in the `turn` item and in a `turn` entry.
AC3. Answer (2.3): Allow once, Allow `<reach>` this session and Deny… call
     `actions.answer(<ask id>, …)`, and `App` passes that id as `answer`'s `ask` argument. The
     decision JSON is unchanged (`decisionJson`). `view.openAsks` holds ask ids (asks only, not
     checks), in arrival order; "Needs you (n)" counts them; "Needs you" scrolls to and focuses the
     card of `openAsks[0]` (the card carries `data-ask="<ask id>"`; `data-call` may stay).
AC4. Decisions (9.4): a decision entry (an `ask` entry with `decision`) clears the open ask of the
     same ask `id` only. Two asks or a check and an ask on the same `toolCallId` with different
     ids: deciding one leaves the other open with its bar. `outcome` `none` is accepted (a check
     the checked provider let through) and its line reads "No objection: <title>"; any `by` value
     is shown as text (`owner`, `session-allow`, `checked`, `kernel`, `host`, or one the console
     does not know); an outcome other than allow, deny, none is an `unknown` row. A decided ask's
     line names the call by the ask's `toolCall.title` (else the argument of its `input`, below).
AC5. The card comes from the ask entry only (18.3, 9.8). When the ask arrives, the row's call
     becomes the ask's `toolCall` (its `toolCallId` forced to the ask's) whole, or, without
     `toolCall`, a call with only the id, `kind` `"other"`, no content and no locations: the
     streamed title, kind, locations, diff, rawInput and output of that id are gone from the card.
     The card's argument line comes from the ask's `input` (`file_path`, `path`, `command`, `url`,
     `pattern`, in that order; else none), never from `toolCall.rawInput` or `title`. After the ask
     (open or decided) a later `tool_call`, `tool_call_update` or `tool` entry of the same
     `toolCallId` changes only `status` while the ask is open; after the decision it may also add
     program output (`content` of type `content` or `terminal`, `rawOutput`), never title, kind,
     locations, rawInput or diff content. The card keeps the ask's call and its Input after the
     decision (the "Waiting for you" tag and the bar go; the Input stays, labelled "Input").
AC6. Folding: a call with an open or decided ask is never folded into "Explored", also when its
     kind is read or search and a later update arrives.
AC7. Check (9.8, 18.6): a `kind: "check"` ask is a card tagged "Checking" with its Input, no ask
     bar, no buttons that answer, not in `openAsks`, not in "Needs you", not the status line's
     subject. Its decision is a decision line like an ask's.
AC8. Verdict (9.4): a `verdict` with `on` and no `toolCallId` (`on: "input"`) is a decision line
     "<outcome>: the input"; with `toolCallId`, as today. A verdict without `by` or `outcome` stays
     unreadable.
AC9. The fake (`console/fake/story.ts`) writes the S10 wire: the ask entry has `id` (say

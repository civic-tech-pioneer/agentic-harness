## Goal

A skewed clock can no longer make a read wait longer than its own interval (S7 RISK d). A session
that pages in its history says "Loading" for its turn, never "Idle", until it knows the turn. And
the sidebar puts the open session under "Needs you" whenever the dock shows "Needs you (n)", so the
two never disagree (the PO saw the long fixture under "Running" with "Needs you (1)").

## Acceptance

AC1. Governor clamp. In `governor()` (`kit/kernel.ts`), the elapsed time used to refill is
     clamped to `0 .. (BURST / REFILL_PER_S) * 1000` ms: a clock that jumps back (now < last)
     adds no tokens and never makes a waiting call wait more than `1000 / REFILL_PER_S` ms past
     its token's due time; a clock that jumps forward refills at most to `BURST`. A unit test with
     a fake `Date.now` moved back by an hour shows the next queued call runs within 250 ms (fake
     timers), and the burst of 8 and the 4/s rate still hold (existing `kit/governor.test.ts`).
AC2. Loading. `SessionView` gains `loading: boolean` (in `emptyView` false). `openSession` sets it
     true from an attach that answers `behind` until the pages are in (`pageIn` done, success or
     failure), and false otherwise. While `loading` and the view has no `turn` (or the turn is
     not yet known), the status line reads "Loading" with its own shape (`data-shape="loading"`,
     aria-label "Loading", a CSS shape distinct from the others in 18.5), never "Idle". A test on
     the long fixture (`fake/long.ts`) samples the status word while it pages in: never "Idle".
AC3. Sidebar agrees. `Sidebar` takes `needs?: boolean`; App passes `needs={view.openAsks.length > 0}`.
     The open session's row is in the "Needs you" group with the `g-ask` glyph whenever `needs` is
     true, whatever the turn says; else as today. A test renders the long fixture's state (turn
     `running`, one open ask) and finds the open session under "Needs you". The hold while the
     pointer is over the list (18.6) still holds.
AC4. `npm test` stays within 14.5 s on this machine, or grows less than 5% from its time at the
     start of the task (report both).

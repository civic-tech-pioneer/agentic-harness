## Goal
Make the queue, composer, and turn indicators describe what the participant can do, and make Stage D distinguish measured delivery loss from a batch cut by Stop using a shared clock and durable evidence.

## Acceptance
1. AC1: The consent shape stays beside its words at 390 px and 1280 px. A taken input's refusal has its own full-width line and is announced once.
2. AC2: Empty input shows a hint only after an attempted send. Reported thinking reads Thinking. Esc cannot stop an inactive turn. Paused and failed queue rows explain when inputs run and only offer Stop and send during active turns. Removed inputs retain their specified Discard path.
3. AC3: Browser observations and Stop use the wall clock. A complete batch can be excluded only near both the click and the stored interrupt. Real paced replies write the same marker in their live delta and their persisted message, without double counting.

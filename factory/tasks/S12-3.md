## Goal

The Claude adapter (`kernel/src/adapters/claude.rs`) does what TECHNICAL 9.8 "Code to change
(S12)" and the "Kernel, S12" lines of 9.4 "Code to change" say. An ask or check becomes one `ask`
entry in the 9.4 shape (`toolCallId`, `toolCall` built from the item's `tool` and `input` only,
`input`, `expires-at` from the item), with no separate `ask` update and no `options`. A host
`ask-resolved` for an expiry or cancel becomes a decision entry that repeats the ask. Items that
could pass as an ask but are malformed, wrapped, or from a provider become `unknown`. A finished
agent message carries its `thought`, and every live update carries `_meta.watane.at`.

## Acceptance

AC1. An `ask` item `{"type":"ask","id":"a1","tool_use_id":"toolu_1","tool":"Bash","input":{...},
     "expires-at":1763600060000}` gives one `Out` with no `update`, no `watane`, and an entry
     `kind: ask`, body exactly `{"id":"a1","kind":"ask","toolCallId":"toolu_1","toolCall":
     {"toolCallId":"toolu_1","title":"Bash","rawInput":{...}},"input":{...},"expires-at":1763600060000}`.
     A `check` item gives the same with `"kind":"check"`. No `reach` (the broker adds it,
     9.6; it is absent when none is offered). `toolCall` is never copied from an earlier
     `tool_use` of the same id: a `tool_use` of id `toolu_1` with another input before the ask
     leaves the ask's `toolCall.rawInput` equal to the ask item's `input`.
AC2. `expires-at` is the item's; the adapter adds nothing to `at` (`ASK_TTL_MS` goes).
AC3. An `ask` or `check` item whose `tool_use_id` is missing, null, not a string or `""`, whose
     `id` is missing or null, whose `tool` is not a string, or whose `expires-at` is not a JSON
     number, is one `unknown` entry `{"raw": <the item>}`, never an ask.
AC4. `ask-resolved {id, by}` where `id` is an ask or check the adapter has seen and `by` is
     `expired` or `cancelled`: an `ask` entry that repeats every body field of that ask and adds
     `"decision":{"outcome":"deny","by":"host","reason":"<by>"}`; the open ask is forgotten. With
     any other `by` (the host confirming an owner's or checked provider's answer, whose decision
     entry the kernel writes when it answers, 9.4): no `Out`, the open ask is forgotten. With an
     `id` the adapter does not hold: one `unknown` entry. A second `ask-resolved` of the same id:
     `unknown`.
AC5. `{"type":"wrapped",...}`, `{"type":"verdict",...}`, `{"type":"checking",...}` items are each
     one `unknown` entry (the adapter has no checked-provider mode yet; 10 is a later slice).
AC6. A line that is not valid UTF-8 is one `unknown` entry whose `raw` is the line as a string
     (U+FFFD for bad bytes, display only); it is never parsed as JSON. A line that is not one JSON
     object stays `unknown` as today.
AC7. `system` lines with `subtype` `hook_started`, `hook_response`, and top-level `type`
     `tool_progress` or `task_progress` (or the `system` subtypes of those names; check
     `proto/claude-probes/` transcripts for their real shape) give no `Out`.
AC8. Every `Out` with an `update` has `update._meta.watane.at` = the `at` passed to `line`
     (merge with an existing `_meta`, such as the cut marker on a tool result).
AC9. Thinking (from `assistant` `thinking` blocks and `thinking_delta` stream events) is
     collected per message: the finished agent `message` entry's body gains
     `"thought":{"text":"<the thinking texts of that step joined, in order>","seconds":<n>}`, `n`
     = (at of the last thought chunk − at of the first) / 1000, rounded to the nearest whole
     second. A message with no thinking has no `thought`. The collected thinking is cleared when
     the message entry is made (and at a `result`).
AC10. The golden fixtures in `kernel/tests/fixtures/adapters/claude/` are updated to these shapes
     (`asks.json` especially), and a fixture or test covers each AC.
AC11. `cargo test` and clippy (as `factory/gate.sh`) pass.

## Interface

`ClaudeAdapter::new()` and `ClaudeAdapter::line(&mut self, line: &[u8], at: u64) -> Vec<Out>`
keep their signatures. `Out`, `Entry`, `Kind` keep their shapes (no new `Kind`). No sibling uses
the adapter.

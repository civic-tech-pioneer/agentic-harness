## Acceptance

AC1. `npm run test:host` in `console/` with the 4f105a9 programs on `PATH` serves the kernel and
     prints the served page URL; with a `watane-dev` that refuses `watane:host/streams` (v0.1.0) it
     prints one line naming the cause and exits 2 (not 1: not a failed case). It never touches the
     real watane home (`WATANE_HOME` or `--home` in a temp dir) and stops its preview at the end
     (`watane-dev serve --stop`), also on failure.
AC2. live: the console opens a probe session (or starts one) and shows its numbered items arriving,
     in order, none doubled, within 2 s of each firing (probe `every-ms` 200 or less).
AC3. reload: after `page.reload()` the page shows every item once (1..n contiguous, no gap, no
     double) and new ones keep arriving.
AC4. replaced (51): a second page on the same session; the first one's stream ends `replaced`
     (seen as the `stream-end` message, or the console's state) and the second stays live; items
     in the second are contiguous.
AC5. lagging (36 kernel part, 51): a consumer that stops reading. Use a raw HTTP client on
     `/events` (read the `connection` message, call `sessions.attach` over `/call` with that
     `connection`, then stop reading the socket) while the probe pushes items; the stream ends
     (`lagging`, or the connection ends past 16 MiB: S96's user-sim saw `changed` and then the
     response end with no `stream-end`; record which one this host does). A reattach with `after`
     = the last index read misses and doubles nothing (check with `sessions.read`). If the probe's
     caps (9.9) cannot reach the host's in-flight limit or 16 MiB, say so in the output and check
     the reattach after the connection is closed by the client instead.
AC6. case 20: the host process (`watane --preview`, its pid from the preview's folder or `ps`) is
     killed while a probe firing is due; after 5 s it is started again (`watane-dev serve` again,
     reusing the preview's home), and the due firing comes within 2 s of start (the next probe
     item appears through `sessions.read`); the firing of a cancelled or deleted probe is ignored
     (delete a probe session, restart, no new item, no error in `watane.log`).
AC7. case 21: 10 `kill -9`s of the host at random points of a running probe, each followed by a
     restart; then `sessions.read` gives a contiguous transcript (no gap, no double) and the probe
     goes on to its `count`.
AC8. Every check prints `PASS <name>` or `FAIL <name>: <what it saw>`; case 7 and the defer half of
     36 print `SKIP`. The run ends with one line `stage A on watane 4f105a9: <p> pass, <f> fail, <s> skip`.
AC9. `factory/gate.sh` passes. The script is not in the gate nor in `test:slow` (CI installs the
     v0.1.0 watane-dev). `AGENTS.md`'s Tests table gets a row for it (by hand, like the probes), and
     TECHNICAL 16 or 18.7 names it in one line.

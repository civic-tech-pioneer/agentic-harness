## Goal

The agent kernel is a watane mod: `wit/world.wit` (package `mods:agent@0.1.0`) imports host 19's
`state`, `caller`, `streams` and `timers`, exports the kernel's `sessions` (2.3) and `probe` (9.9)
and the host-only `stream-sink`, `stream-producer` and `timer-sink`; the wrapper crate
`kernel/wasm/` (`agent-mod`, cdylib) implements the crate's `Host` and `State` over those imports
and builds `logic.wasm` at the root (prebuilt logic, watane dev 7.1), which `factory/gate.sh`
builds before `check-mod.sh`. A page that calls `probe.start` then `sessions.attach` with its
connection gets the probe's numbered entries on its own stream.

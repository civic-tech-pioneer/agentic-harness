Slice: S29 — ending a turn never fails at the ceiling

## Goal

At and past the kernel's ceiling (the account plus the reserve, 256 MiB by
default), every call that ends a turn or closes a session's work succeeds
and frees what it holds, charged into the reserve: `cancel`, `interrupt`
without a replacement, a provider item while past (among them the one that
ends an active turn `stopped {limit: quota}` and sends
`Request::Interrupt`), and the provider stream's `ended` (already so since
S28). Today `cancel` and the quota-stop item fail `quota` at the ceiling and
commit nothing, so the session keeps running, its `claude` keeps its
provider slot, and (once an `ended` took the account above the ceiling)
another session's item that should stop its turn is dropped (S28 RISKs,
PR #32). New work stays refused at the ceiling: `start`, `say`, an
`interrupt` replacement, `rename`.

## Acceptance

Set the ceiling as `s28.rs` does: `k.limits.account_bytes` = the account's
current bytes, `k.limits.reserve_bytes = 0`.

AC1. `cancel` at the ceiling on a `waiting` session with a live provider
stream, one open ask and one pending answer: returns `Ok`; both asks are
closed (two decision entries), the turn ends `idle {interrupted}` (one
`turn` entry), the provider slot (`slots`, `quota/<owner>` provider count)
and its `stream/` and `live/` keys go as below the ceiling, the session's
deadlines and timer are gone (`slots.timers` and the owner's `timers` down),
the session leaves `active/`, `Request::Cancel` is sent; `quota/<owner>`,
`quota` and the record's `bytes` each changed by exactly what the call wrote
(as `s28.rs` ac2 measures).

AC2. `interrupt` without a replacement at the ceiling on a `running`
session: `Ok`, turn `idle {interrupted}`, its `Request::Interrupt` sent,
charged exactly as AC1. `interrupt` with a replacement at the ceiling still
fails `quota` and commits nothing.

AC3. At the ceiling, a running session gets a provider item that would be
stored (a `message` entry): the call returns `Ok`, the turn ends `idle
{stopped {limit: quota}}` (one `turn` entry), `Request::Interrupt` is sent,
the message is not stored, and the accounts are charged exactly.

AC4. "Above the ceiling persists": after an `ended` of session A took the
account above the ceiling, session B's (other owner or same) provider item
still ends B's turn `stopped {limit: quota}` and sends its
`Request::Interrupt` (AC3 on an account already above).

AC5. At the ceiling, a provider `ask` item on a running session: `Ok`; the
ask is not stored and is denied at once (`Request::Answer` deny, reason
`quota`); the turn ends `stopped {limit: quota}` with `Request::Interrupt`
(the first item past quota on an active turn, 9.3 "Any call when past").

AC6. Still refused at the ceiling, committing nothing: `say`, `start`,
`rename`, an `answer` with `for: session`; and below the reserve every
existing quota test passes (only the test in "Changed tests" changes).

AC7. A provider item while NOT past at the start of the call is not let past
the ceiling: the existing `quota.rs`
`ac9_a_call_cannot_take_the_account_past_the_reserve` and `idle.rs` ac9
tests pass unchanged.

AC8. `cargo test` and `factory/gate.sh` pass; `cargo clippy` and
`cargo fmt --check` clean.

## Spec

The rules, quoted (the foreman wrote them in this slice's first commit):

- 9.3 Quota *Past.* (the ceiling rule): "No call takes the account past 256
  MiB: one that would fails `quota` and, as every failed call, commits
  nothing (7). The exceptions cannot fail at the ceiling, since each ends a
  turn or closes a session's work and frees what it holds: a firing of the
  restart pass, a provider stream's `ended`, `cancel`, `interrupt` without a
  replacement, and a provider item while past (among them the one that ends
  the turn `stopped {limit: quota}` and sends `Request::Interrupt`). As built
  (S29): what they wrote, even a restart step that failed, is charged to both
  accounts and the record's `bytes`, and what they write is bounded in bytes:
  at most 64 decisions and one `turn` entry beyond the record and the keys
  they delete, and every decision written while past is short (9.4) ... A
  provider item while past stores nothing else (below)."
- 9.3 Quota *Past.*, the flag: "Each is read before the call writes (as
  built (S28): the flag lives only in the accounts the call read, never
  between calls)". "A provider item while past" means `Past::is_past()` of
  the accounts that call read; an item that starts below quota keeps the
  ceiling check.
- 9.3 Quota, *Any call when past*: "a new ask is not stored and is denied at
  once (`Request::Answer` deny, reason `quota`); any other entry is not
  stored. When the session's turn is active, the first such item ends it
  `stopped {limit: quota}` (its `turn` entry from the reserve) and sends
  `Request::Interrupt`; queued inputs stay queued and no queued input starts
  while past."
- 9.3 *Refused `quota` when past*: "`start`, `say` (whether it starts a turn
  or queues), an `interrupt` replacement, `rename`, an `answer` with `for:
  session`."
- 16 case 77 (whole).
- The model in code: `provider_work` passes `ended.is_some()` as
  `cannot_fail` to `finish_as` (S28); the restart firing cannot fail either.
- Where the spec is unclear, stop and say so in your last message.

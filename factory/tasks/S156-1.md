## Goal
After Alice stops a turn, the transcript says what happened in her words: the footer counts only the
tool calls that ran, a kernel-closed ask reads "Ask cancelled … — the turn was stopped" (not
"kernel · Denied … — cancel"), a card she allowed that never ran reads "Did not run", and the footer
reads as the frame's divider. The status line says "Thinking" while the agent's last act is a thought.
## Acceptance
AC1 A turn ends `interrupted` after asks that never ran (decided or not): the footer's tool count leaves
    them out ("Stopped after 3 s · 0 tools").
AC2 A decision `{deny, by: kernel, reason: cancel}` shows "Ask cancelled · Edit <file> — the turn was
    stopped"; `restart` "— watane restarted", `ended` "— the connection to Claude Code ended",
    `busy` "— too many open asks"; any other kernel reason shows as before. Its card's badge reads
    "Cancelled" (b-off) for `cancel`.
AC3 An ask allowed whose call is still `pending` when the turn ends reads "Did not run" (b-off), not
    "Allowed"; before the turn ends it still reads "Allowed".
AC4 A running turn whose last row is a thought (streaming, or folded with nothing after it) says
    "Thinking"; with no row of the turn yet, "Working" (TECHNICAL 18.5, S35).
AC5 The footer line is the frame's centred divider (Main-2 `.footer`).

## Goal

The stage F budgets of TECHNICAL 17 that this cloud can measure are measured (sketch outcome 5): the
state bytes read and written per step of a root with two `task` children, each child one step with
two tool jobs, at 10 and at 1,000 steps of history, on the fake host, as S128's stage D test does.
The page-level budgets (item delay, Stop through the last child, writer busy share) need `watane-dev`
and the scripted Responses server, which this cloud lacks; their runner lives in
`console/test/host/` (S131's folder until it merges), so they are recorded as not run.

## Acceptance

AC1. `cargo test s134_stage_f_state_bytes_per_step -- --ignored --nocapture` in `kernel/` prints,
     for 10 and 1,000 steps, the bytes read and written per root step and per child step, and fails
     when a step exceeds 4 MiB or the 1,000-step figure exceeds the 10-step one by more than 10%
     (17, row F "as D").
AC2. The test drives the stage F fixture of 17: 2 owners, 1 root each, 2 simultaneous children per
     root, each child one step with at most 2 open tool jobs, and a Stop of each root with live
     children (all stopped; counted calls).
AC3. TECHNICAL 16 "Budget record" gets a stage F row with the date, the machine (`nproc`, OS), the
     measured bytes, and "page-level budgets not run: no watane-dev here".

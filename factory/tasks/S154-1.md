Slice: S154 — Close asks when interrupt cancels a Claude launch

## Goal
Stopping a still-launching Claude session records a final decision for every ask on the cancelled stream, even if a replacement starts.

## Acceptance
1. AC1: Feed numbered asks before initialization, answer one, then interrupt with or without a replacement. The old stream is cancelled, the unanswered ask is denied, the accepted answer is preserved, and the old ask deadlines are gone.

## Spec
TECHNICAL 9.3 (interrupt and cancel), 9.8 (provider termination), 14 (session privacy and consent).

## Interface
Existing `Kernel::interrupt(caller: &str, session: &str, replacement: Option<&str>) -> Result<Called<()>, KernelError>` and provider `Request::Cancel`.

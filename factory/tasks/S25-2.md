## Goal

The kernel holds every owner to 9.3 "Quota" as its "As built (S25)" bullets
say: each call charges its writes to the owner (`quota/<owner>`) and the
kernel's account (`quota`); an owner past 64 sessions or 64 MiB (kernel
settings), or the account past 224 MiB, gets `quota` for new sessions and new
input; a running turn ends `stopped {limit: quota}` from the reserve, and only
decisions, turn changes and deletes go on; `delete` frees a session's bytes
without reading its chunks, while kept `key/` results stay charged; an owner
whose cap is set past 256 sessions gets more `owner/<owner>/<n>` pages; a
session at the chunk cap ends its turn the same way (case 53). Cases 53 and
59 prove it. Guard: an owner must not fill the state other owners need.

## Acceptance

AC1. **Settings.** `Kernel` gains `pub limits: Limits` (`Limits::default()`
from `Kernel::new`) and its `state` field becomes `Metered<S>`
(`Kernel::new(state, host)` keeps its signature; tests keep using
`k.state.reset_counts()`, `k.state.counts()`, `Slots::load(&mut k.state)`).
`segment` of `sessions.rs` is `quota::segment`.

AC2. **Exact bytes.** After any sequence of `start`, `say`, `answer`,
`interrupt`, `cancel`, `unqueue`, `rename`, `provider` items, restart steps
and `delete` by two owners, `quota/<owner>.bytes` of each equals the sum of
`key.len() + value.len()` over the keys charged to that owner (9.3 "Bytes";
count them from `MemState::entries()` by scope, `key/<seg>/` and
`owner/<seg>/` by the owner's segment, session keys by the record's owner),
and `quota.bytes` the sum over both owners. `quota/<owner>.sessions` and
`quota.sessions` count the live records. A call that changes no charged
byte (a replayed key, a refused call, `read`, `list`, `queued`) writes
neither account.

AC3. **Record.** `Record` gains `owner-page` (u32, default 0) and `bytes`
(u64, default 0), the serde names as written. `bytes` always equals the sum
of the session's `Scope::Session` keys (AC2's test checks it per session).

AC4. **Delete frees.** `delete` deletes the session's chunks and queue with
`delete_uncharged`, frees the record's `bytes` plus the record (by `adjust`
and the charged record delete), decrements `sessions` in both accounts, and
reads no chunk (assert no `entries/` key in `get_keys()`). Its kept
`key/<owner>/<key>` results stay and stay charged (case 59): after deleting
every session, the owner's `bytes` equals the bytes of its `key/` keys.

AC5. **Owner sessions.** With 64 live sessions the 65th `start` is refused
`quota` and writes nothing; after one `delete` a `start` succeeds. With
`k.limits.owner_sessions = 300`: the 257th session goes to
`owner/<seg>/1` (its record's `owner-page` is 1, `quota/<owner>.pages` is
`[256, 1]`); `list` pages through all 300 in start order with `after`;
`delete` of a page-1 session reads `owner/<seg>/1` and not page 0; a page
that empties is deleted and its count stays 0, and the next `start` fills
the first page with room; the 301st `start` is refused `quota`.

AC6. **Owner bytes.** With `k.limits.owner_bytes` small enough that a few
inputs pass it: once `quota/<owner>.bytes >= owner_bytes`, `start`, `say` (on
an idle session and on a running one, which would queue), `interrupt` with a
replacement, `rename` and `answer` with `for: session` are each refused
`quota` and write nothing (snapshot); `answer` without `for`, `interrupt`
without a replacement, `cancel`, `unqueue`, `delete`, `read`, `list`,
`queued` still work. Another owner is not affected. After `delete` of the
owner's sessions brings it under, `start` works again.

AC7. **The account.** Same as AC6 for `k.limits.account_bytes` reached by
two owners together, each under its own cap: both are refused `quota`.

AC8. **A running turn stops.** When the owner or the account is past (as
read at the start of the provider call), the session's turn is `running` or
`waiting`, and an item with a non-control entry arrives (`message`, `tool`,
`unknown`, ...): that entry is not stored; the turn becomes `idle {stopped
{limit: quota}}` (one `turn` entry, stored and pushed); `Request::Interrupt`
is sent; the session leaves `active/`; queued inputs stay queued and none
starts. Later non-control items for that session store nothing and send
nothing. A new ask past quota is not stored, the turn stops as above when
active, and `Request::Answer {deny, reason "quota"}` is sent for it. An ask
decision entry, an `ask-resolved` and a `ProviderEvent::Turn` are stored as
before; a turn that ends while past starts no queued input.

AC9. **The reserve ceiling.** A call whose writes would take `quota.bytes`
past `account_bytes + reserve_bytes` returns `quota` (the host commits
nothing of a failed call, 7; the test asserts the error only).

AC10. **Chunk cap (case 53).** A session whose record holds 16 384 chunk
starts minus the room for one `turn` entry: a provider `message` item is not
stored, the turn ends `stopped {limit: quota}`, the `turn` entry is stored;
new input (`say`) is refused `quota`.

AC11. **Bounds kept.** A provider item, `say` and `answer` each read at
most `quota/<owner>` and `quota` more than before and write each at most
once; the meter reads no key the call did not already read, except keys new
to the call (a new chunk, a new key result) and the stale `stream/` keys a
restart step deletes.

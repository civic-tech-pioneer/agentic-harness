## Goal
After the host's boot number changes, the kernel finds the turns that nothing drives and settles
them, one session per call, without a prefix scan (TECHNICAL 9.3 "Restart", R1–R6): Claude
`running`/`waiting` sessions become `unknown` with their open asks denied, `paused` ones stay,
`say` on `unknown` resumes, and any call that reads a stale record steps it first. Cases 67's
restart neighbours 68, 69 and 71 of 16 are kernel tests; case 70 (OpenAI) waits for stage D.

## Acceptance
AC1 R1: every top-level call (`start`, `say`, `answer`, `queued`, `unqueue`, `read`, `rename`,
    `delete`, `list`, `timer`) reads `slots` and calls `Host::boot` (a `HostCall::Boot` is
    recorded); `provider` does neither. When they differ the call sets `slots.boot`, zeroes
    `provider`, `request` and `job`, keeps `timers`, sets `slots.restart = Some(0)` and records
    `TimerAfter { id: "restart", delay_ms: 0 }`; it steps no session it does not read (R6).
AC2 R2 (case 68): with 1 024 listed sessions of the old boot (seeded through `start` or
    `active::add` plus records), firings of `timer("restart")` step exactly one session each and
    re-arm `restart` with delay 0; the firing after the last stale one records
    `TimerCancel { id: "restart" }` and sets `restart` to `None`; so `A` stale sessions take at
    most `A + 1` firings. Each firing reads at most the 4 `active/` pages plus its session's
    step (count `MemState` gets and bytes: pages at most 96 KiB). A second boot change during
    the pass restarts it from page 0 and steps entries of any older boot. After a boot change
    with no call, no session changes.
AC3 R4 step (case 69, Claude): the session's `stream/<id>` keys of `record.streams` and
    `live/<session>` are deleted and `streams` emptied; `record.boot` and the page entry's boot
    become `slots.boot`. A Claude `running` or `waiting` session: each open ask in `open` gets a
    decision entry (the ask entry read at its `i`, plus `decision {outcome: "deny", by:
    "kernel", reason: "restart"}`), its `ask <id>` deadline is dropped, `open` empties, the turn
    becomes `unknown` (a `turn` entry; the push `{"type":"turn","state":{"state":"unknown"}}`
    returned by the call that steps it), it leaves `active/` and the queue is kept. A tool call
    streamed with no `tool` entry gets none. `paused` (either backend): unchanged but for the
    boot. `idle`/`unknown`/`resume-lost` (reached only by R6): only the stale keys go. An OpenAI
    `running` or `waiting` session: only the stale keys and the boot change (its 9.5 rows wait
    for stage D; say so in a comment and in your report).
AC4 R3: one step writes at most the record, its page (or `slots` and the page when it leaves the
    index), one decision entry per open ask plus one `turn` entry (the transcript's chunk writes),
    and deletes the stale keys; a test counts it.
AC5 R5: `say` on `unknown` records `Request::Turn { resume: true }` and the turn is `running`; a
    `ProviderEvent::Turn(ResumeLost)` then gives `resume-lost`. With inputs queued, the `say`
    input joins the end of the queue and the turn starts with the oldest (its `Request::Turn`
    carries the oldest input, `resume: true`).
AC6 R6 (case 71): after a boot change, each of `read`, `say`, `answer`, `delete` (and `queued`,
    `unqueue`, `rename`) on a stale session does its step first in the same call and acts on the
    new state: `read` shows the denial and `unknown` entries; `answer` to an ask the step denied
    is refused with one refusal `{field: "ask"}`; then the pass skips that session (its page
    entry is current). `list` over stale sessions reads no record beyond the ones it lists,
    reports `{"state":"unknown"}` for a stale Claude `running`/`waiting` one, and writes nothing
    (no put, no delete).

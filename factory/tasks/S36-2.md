## Goal

A session's timer firing never stops short (PLAN "Stage B carry list", S34 RISK 3). Today
`session_firing` (`kernel/src/sessions.rs`) returns at `self.settle(..)?` when the restart step that
`settle` runs fails, before it re-arms the session's timer and before it charges what it wrote; and a
due firing that ran no step calls `finish_as(.., cannot_fail: stepped)`, so at the account's ceiling
it fails `quota` and the idle release it did is lost. After this task a firing is never an error in
effect, as `restart_firing` already is (R2): it always leaves the session's timer set for its next
due deadline, and what it wrote is charged to the record's `bytes` and both accounts, past the
reserve too (9.3 "Past": the calls that cannot fail).

## Acceptance

AC1. A Claude session of an older boot with a due `idle` deadline and a live provider stream, whose
     restart step fails (make a state write of the step fail in the crate's test host, as
     `restart_firing`'s tests do): the firing re-arms `s/<id>` for the next due deadline (a
     `HostCall::TimerAfter { id: "s/<id>", .. }` is recorded) and the bytes the failed step wrote are in
     the record's `bytes` and the owner's and the kernel's accounts (as `restart_firing` charges a
     failed step); a later call on the session still does its restart step (R6).
AC2. A due `idle` firing with no restart step, with the kernel's account at the ceiling (account plus
     reserve): the firing releases the provider stream (a `Request::Cancel` for it, the slot freed in
     `slots`), drops the `idle` deadline, and commits (the record after it has no `idle` deadline and
     no stream); nothing returns `quota`.
AC3. A firing with nothing due and no step still writes nothing (existing tests unchanged).
AC4. `timer()` keeps returning `Called<()>`; the existing tests of `timers_s34.rs`, `idle.rs`,
     `restart.rs`, `s29_ceiling.rs` pass unchanged.

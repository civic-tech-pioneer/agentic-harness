## Goal

Served by watane, the console runs a real Claude session instead of the stage A probe. It opens the
owner's newest session, or, with none (and on New session), the new-session view narrowed to stage
B: profile `scratch`, "A new scratch folder", a composer. Her first message starts the session
(`sessions.start`) and the page opens it: her message on the right, the fake `claude`'s text
streaming in ("Writing…" until the entry), folded thinking, the status line Working then Idle, and
after a reload the same transcript, nothing doubled. The S32 "felt wrong" items of the console are
fixed: no probe composer, an alert in words instead of the raw error, typed text kept across a
reload, no blank flash after a reload, no "(18.8)" shown or read to the person. The stage A host
script becomes the stage B one.

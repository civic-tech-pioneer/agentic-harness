## Goal
The queue and what it took look like Main-2 at 390 px and at desktop width: each queued row one line
of text with an ellipsis (a 255 KiB message too), quiet 500-weight buttons, a muted ×; at phone width
the controls go under the text instead of squeezing it. A taken row is one line (text, then its buttons),
its error at most two lines under it. Stop and send is not offered while the session is not running
(`unknown` or `resume-lost`).
## Acceptance
AC1 At 390 and 1440 px a queued row's text is one line (height of one line, `text-overflow: ellipsis`),
    and the row is no taller than text line + one 36 px control line + padding.
AC2 Queue buttons are 500 weight, transparent; the × is muted and has aria "Remove from queue".
AC3 A taken row (Removed, or a refused Stop and send) keeps its text on one line and its buttons beside or
    below it; the error is clamped to two lines; at 390 px the whole item is at most 4 lines tall; the
    error shows once (not also above the composer).
AC4 Turn `resume-lost`: no Stop and send on queued rows nor on a taken row (as `unknown` already).
AC5 "Enter a message first." only after an empty send; no Discard beside an ordinary Undo (re-checked:
    done on main; pinned by the slow check).

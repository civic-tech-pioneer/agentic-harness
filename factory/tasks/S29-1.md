Slice: S29 — ending a turn never fails at the ceiling

## Goal

Past quota, a decision entry no longer copies its ask. Every ask or check
decision the kernel writes while the owner or the kernel's account is past
(counted at that decision's write, with the bytes the call has written so
far) is the short form of 9.4 "The short decision":
`{"id","kind","ask-i","decision"}`, built from the record alone (the `open`
or `pending-answers` entry), without reading the ask entry. Below quota the
decision repeats the ask, as today. So what a closing call writes past quota
is bounded in bytes: at most one full decision (the one that crossed) and the
rest short, together within the record's size plus 16 KiB, and one `turn`
entry. This is the byte bound the S28 RISK asked for; S29-2 relies on it
when it lets more calls pass the ceiling.

## Acceptance

AC1. A `waiting` session (one open ask, one pending answer) of an owner past
its quota (or the kernel's account past, either one) gets the provider
stream's `ended`: both decision entries have exactly the keys `id`, `kind`,
`ask-i`, `decision` in their body; each `ask-i` is the index of the ask entry
with the same `id`; the open one's `decision` is
`{"outcome":"deny","by":"kernel","reason":"ended"}`, the pending one's is its
pending answer (as `PendingAnswer::decision()`, `input` included).

AC2. The same with `cancel` (reason `cancel`) and with the restart step (R4,
reason `restart`, through `timer("restart")` after a boot change) while past:
short decisions, `ask-i` right.

AC3. A host `ask-resolved` item while past (an open ask resolved `expired`,
and a pending one `answered` with its outcome) writes a short decision.

AC4. Below quota every decision is the full form, unchanged: the existing
tests that read decision bodies (`restart.rs`, `asks.rs`, `s28.rs`,
`transcript.rs`) pass unchanged.

AC5. Crossing within a call: a session with several open asks whose ask
entries are large (each well over 10 KiB) and an owner just below its byte
cap (set `limits.owner_bytes` so the first full decision takes it over):
a closing call (`ended` or `cancel`) writes the first decision(s) full and,
after the call's own writes reach the cap, the rest short.

AC6. The bound: with 64 open or pending asks (ask entries each over 100 KiB)
on an owner past quota, `ended` writes under 1 MiB of entries in all
(measure the change of the session's `entries/` keys).

AC7. Past quota, an ask entry from the adapter that already carries a
`decision` is stored short when its `id` is open or pending (and closes it as
today), and is not stored otherwise. No adapter makes one today; drive it
through `provider` with an `Out` entry as `quota.rs`
`ac8_decisions_resolutions_and_turns_are_stored_while_past` does, and keep
that test passing.

AC8. `cargo test` and `factory/gate.sh` pass; `cargo clippy` and
`cargo fmt --check` clean.

## Spec

The rules, quoted (the foreman wrote them in this slice's first commit):

- 9.4 "The short decision" (S29), whole paragraph: "A decision written while
  the owner or the kernel's account is past (9.3 "Past"; counted at the
  decision's write, with the bytes the call has written so far, so a call
  that crosses the cap writes the decisions after the crossing short) does
  not repeat the ask. Its body is `{"id":"<ask id>","kind":"ask"|"check",
  "ask-i":<i>,"decision":{...}}`. `ask-i` is the index of the ask entry (the
  `i` of the ask's `open` or `pending-answers` entry, 9.3); `decision` is as
  above, a pending answer's `input` included. The kernel writes it from the
  record alone, without reading the ask entry. ... Past quota, an ask entry
  that already carries a decision (no adapter makes one today, 9.8) is
  stored short when its `id` is open or pending, and is not stored
  otherwise."
- 9.3 Quota *Past.* (the ceiling rule): "No call takes the account past 256
  MiB: one that would fails `quota` and, as every failed call, commits
  nothing (7). The exceptions cannot fail at the ceiling ... a firing of the
  restart pass, a provider stream's `ended`, `cancel`, `interrupt` without a
  replacement, and a provider item while past ... what they write is bounded
  in bytes: at most 64 decisions and one `turn` entry beyond the record and
  the keys they delete, and every decision written while past is short
  (9.4), so once past a call writes at most one full decision (the one that
  crossed, at most the size of its ask entry) and the rest short (together
  within the record's 512 KiB plus 16 KiB ...)."
- 9.3 Quota *Past.*, the flag: "Each is read before the call writes (as
  built (S28): the flag lives only in the accounts the call read, never
  between calls)". `Past` in `kernel/src/quota.rs` is made only by
  `Past::load` from the two accounts it reads; keep it so. To count "with
  the bytes the call has written so far", let `Past::load` keep the room it
  read (the smaller of the owner's and the account's room below their caps,
  0 when past) and compare it with the open meter's change
  (`Metered::change().owner`) at the decision's write. A new field or method
  on `Past` is fine; a `past` field on `Kernel` is not.
- 9.3, `open` and `pending-answers`: each holds the ask entry's `i` and
  `kind`; `pending-answers` holds `outcome`, `by`, `for?`, `reason?`,
  `input?`.
- Where the spec is unclear, stop and say so in your last message.

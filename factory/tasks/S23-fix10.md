## Goal

A refused answer never puts focus on an answer button. S23-fix8's AC5 (focus to Allow once after a
refusal) is withdrawn as a guard breach (polish against guard: guard wins). After a refusal other
than `{field: "ask"}`, focus goes to the card's refused line (`role="alert"` or status text with
`tabIndex=-1`, no action on Enter), and only when focus is inside that card or on `document.body`;
focus anywhere else (the composer, another card) is never moved. Sending an answer closes the deny
row and clears its reason, so a refused deny comes back as the plain bar.

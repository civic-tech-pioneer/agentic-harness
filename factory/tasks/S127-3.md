## Goal
On a checked provider's stream (`direct: false`) the kernel shows `checking` as the turn's live activity and
writes each `verdict` item as a `verdict` entry; an unsafe or unsure verdict on an input ends the turn
`idle {failed {error: "judge"}}`. On a direct stream both stay `unknown` entries (unchanged).
## Acceptance
AC1. A `{"type":"checking","on":X}` item on a `direct: false` stream, while the turn is `running`, pushes a
     `turn` update `running` with `activity {kind: checking, on: <ask id> | "input"}` (`input` when X is
     `launch` or `input/<n>`); no entry, nothing in the record (live only, 9.3). Not running: nothing.
AC2. A `verdict` item on a `direct: false` stream becomes a `verdict` entry
     `{"on":<ask id>|"input"|"breaker","toolCallId":<id>?,"outcome":…,"reasons":[…],"by":"<provider>","denials-in-a-row":<n>?}`;
     `toolCallId` from the open ask or check with that id (record `open` or `pending-answers`); `by` is the
     provider identity the kernel knows for the session, or `checked` when it knows none. A running turn with
     a `checking` activity is pushed again without it.
AC3. A verdict on `launch` or `input/<n>` whose outcome is `unsafe` or `unsure` ends an active turn
     `idle {failed {error: "judge"}}`; a later `ended` of the stream does not change that turn.
AC4. A verdict or checking item that is not of good shape (on not a non-empty string, outcome not one of the
     three, reasons not a list of strings) is an `unknown` entry. On a `direct: true` stream both remain
     `unknown` (case 64, unchanged).
AC5. `cargo test`, fmt, clippy in `kernel/` pass; the wasm build of the gate passes.

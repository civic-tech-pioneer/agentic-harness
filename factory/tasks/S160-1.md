Slice: S160 — Launch and Stop edges
## Goal
Stop distinguishes an initialized Claude stream from a pending launch across native and component delivery. A successful client read triggers one owed drain.
## Acceptance
AC1. Native initialization advances the cursor and a subsequent Stop interrupts the retained stream.
AC2. A legacy stream with stale consent, or a stream explicitly marked initialized, is interrupted. A current pending launch still cancels and closes its asks.
AC3. An authorized read drains owed work via the client wrapper once; a refused read leaves it owed.

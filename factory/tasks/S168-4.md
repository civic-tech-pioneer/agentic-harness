## Goal
Withdraw removes a client's grant immediately and the kernel timer finishes cancelling affected turns, including children, without page participation.
## Acceptance
1. AC1: withdraw deletes entry and field digests, cancels matching question, and refuses new work immediately.
2. AC2: first batch and timer batches end full cascades within 9.10's bound; re-allow cannot admit old work before completion.
3. AC3: timer completes after page closes or restart and never raises a consent question or model request.
## Spec
VISION boundaries; FUNCTIONAL 3.7, 3.9; TECHNICAL 9.7 Revoking, 9.10 Withdrawing, 14, case 82.
## Interface
Takes S168-2 manager and S168-1 access-standing. Use pub(crate) Kernel::lease, Kernel::cancel_question and manage::{read, put, refusal}; provide Kernel::manage_withdraw and Kernel::withdraw_firing, and a pub(crate) withdrawing guard used by sessions::may_work. State key withdrawing per 9.10.

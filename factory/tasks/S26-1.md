Slice: S26 — the kernel tracks its provider streams

## Goal

The three S25 items the product owner settled (PLAN "Deputy decisions",
2026-11-21 S25 RISKs) hold, and TECHNICAL 9.3 says so: a session can always be
deleted, because `delete` runs no restart step and cancels the session instead
(the host ends its stream and handle and closes its open asks); past quota a
turn gets at most one `turn` entry, so a provider that flaps its turn cannot
grow the transcript without bound; and a refusal caused by the kernel's shared
account says "this mod's agent sessions are at their limit" without telling an
owner that other owners filled the kernel (14, "A session is private").

## Acceptance

AC1. **Delete never steps.** `delete` of a session whose record is of an older
boot (R6 would step it) does no restart step: it writes no entry, no
decision and no turn, and it succeeds even when that step would fail (test: a
stale `waiting` Claude session whose step fails, e.g. a record whose `chunks`
leave no room for the decision entries, or the account at the 256 MiB
ceiling; `delete` returns `Ok` and the record, chunks, queue, `stream/` and
`live/` keys of the session are gone, its `active/` entry removed).

AC2. **Delete cancels.** Every `delete` sends `Request::Cancel {session}` once,
after its last write (as `cancel` does), whatever the turn state; the open
asks get no decision entry from the kernel (the entries are deleted; the host
closes the asks, 4.5). A `delete` that fails (unknown session, not the owner)
sends nothing.

AC3. **One turn entry per turn past quota.** While the owner or the account
is past (as read at the start of the provider call): a `ProviderEvent::Turn`
that ends an active turn is stored (one `turn` entry); any other turn change
from the provider (to `running` or `waiting` from an inactive turn, or one
inactive state to another, e.g. `idle {completed}` after `idle {stopped
{limit: quota}}`) writes no entry and leaves the record's turn as it was.
Test: past quota, a running session gets `Out` (non-control), then
`Turn(idle completed)`, then 100 alternating `Turn(running)` / `Turn(idle)`:
the session has exactly one `turn` entry after the first item, and the 100
items add none.

AC4. **The account refusal names no other owner.** A refusal caused by the
kernel's 224 MiB account (`limits.account_bytes`), or by the state ceiling
(account plus reserve), has the text `this mod's agent sessions are at their
limit` (so `to_string()` is `quota: this mod's agent sessions are at their
limit`), and no text of it contains `kernel`, `account` or a size. Refusals
caused by the owner's own caps keep naming the cap (`64 sessions per owner`,
`64 MiB per owner`, S25-fix1).

AC5. **The spec says so.** TECHNICAL 9.3 is changed in place, and only there
(plus the `R6` sentence that names `delete`): the `delete` bullet ("`delete`
cancels, ...") says delete does no restart step, sends `cancel` (host closes
the asks), and so a session can always be deleted; R6's list of calls that
never act on a stale turn drops `delete`; the Quota "A provider item when
past" bullet says a turn change is stored only when it ends an active turn
(at most one `turn` entry per turn past quota); the "Past" or "Refused" text
says the account cause reads "this mod's agent sessions are at their limit".
`factory/gate.sh` passes (links).

## Goal

The kernel mod links the unchecked provider on watane's host. Its world imports `host-info`,
`claude-session` and the provider's `mods:claude-code/sessions`, and no longer exports `probe`.
Its `Host` calls the provider's `start`, sends each `Request` to `claude-session` (direct stream)
or to the provider (`direct: false`), and takes the host's real generation. `stream-sink.items`
passes each item's raw body to the crate's adapter path, and `ended` passes the outcome as the
cause. Every exported call runs over a `Buffered` state and commits its writes only when the crate
call succeeds (S32 R1), and a contract test on watane's host shows a failed call leaving no write
(it fails on `main` today).

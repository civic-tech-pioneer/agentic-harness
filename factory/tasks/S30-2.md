## Goal

After Alice answers an ask, its card says "Answered, waiting" (S23), but the status line still
reads "Waiting for you" with "denied in m:ss if unanswered" counting down, as if nothing was
answered. After this task the status line agrees with the cards: when every open ask has been
answered by this console, it reads "Answered, waiting" (or "Checking with your judge" when the
card's line says that) with no countdown; when other open asks remain, it names the next one
(as Needs you would go to) and its clock counts down to that ask's own `expires-at`.

## Goal

Past quota the kernel writes a decision that does not repeat its ask (TECHNICAL 9.4 "The short
decision", S29): `{"id","kind","ask-i","decision"}`, with no `toolCallId`, `toolCall`, `input`,
`expires-at` or `reach`. Today the console's wire parser calls that entry malformed and shows a
muted "An event this console does not know" line, while the ask stays open on screen with its
bar. After this task Alice sees a short decision exactly as a full one: the ask closes, its card
keeps the ask's own call and shows the decision, and the decision line names the call by the
ask's `toolCall.title` and shows its input's argument. The card is drawn from the ask entry at
`ask-i`: from the entries the view holds when that one is among them, else by reading that one
entry from the kernel (and joining its parts) — the console never draws a card from anything else.

## Acceptance

AC1. Parse. `kit/wire.ts` reads an `ask` entry whose body has `ask-i` (a positive integer) and a
     well-formed `decision` (as today: `outcome` allow|deny|none, `by` a string, optional `for`,
     `reason`) as a short decision, and needs none of `toolCallId`, `toolCall`, `input`,
     `expires-at`, `reach` for it. `kind` must be `ask` or `check`, `id` a string. An `ask-i` that
     is not a positive integer below the entry's own `i`, or an `ask-i` body without a
     `decision`, is `unreadable` (fail closed). Fields a short decision must not have are ignored
     (a short decision with a stray `toolCallId` is still drawn from `ask-i`, never from the stray).
     A full decision (no `ask-i`) parses exactly as today.
AC2. Held. With the ask entry at index `ask-i` open in the view (same `id`, same index), the short
     decision gives exactly the view a full decision for that ask would give (same rows, same
     card, same decision line text, same `openAsks`, same tool row state), for allow, deny and
     none, for `kind` ask and check, with and without `reason`, and with `for: "session"`. A
     test reduces both and compares the views (row keys aside if they must differ; say why).
AC3. A pending answer. When the console answered the ask ("Answered, waiting"), a short decision
     for it ends that state exactly as a full decision does (App's answered set drops it; the card
     shows the decision; Needs you and the sidebar agree).
AC4. Not held. When the view does not hold the ask entry at `ask-i` (the page lacks it, it is not
     an open ask, or the open ask of that `id` has another index), the short decision still closes
     the `id` at once in reduce order (it leaves `openAsks` and its open card loses its bar in the
     same reduce), and a placeholder decision row stands at the decision's place. `kit/kernel.ts`
     reads that one entry: `read(session, ask-i - 1, 1)`; when the line is a `part` with `m > 1`,
     `read(session, ask-i - m, m)` and join the parts (as `fetchEntry` does for `~p<n>` rows; reuse
     it). The read entry then draws the card and the decision line at the placeholder's place.
     At most one read per short decision; a failed read retries as `fetchEntry` does (3 tries).
AC5. Fail closed on the read. If the read fails, or the entry at `ask-i` is not an `ask` entry of
     the same `id` and `kind` without a decision of its own, or does not parse, the console draws
     no card from it: the decision line shows who and the outcome (and the reason), naming the
     ask by its `id`; no answer bar ever appears for it. Test each.
AC6. Later state wins. While the read is in flight, later entries keep arriving and are reduced
     (the stream is not held up). When the read returns, it touches only the placeholder's row and
     the card it draws; it never reopens or closes an ask, never changes `openAsks`, the answered
     set, the turn or any row of a newer ask that reuses the same `id` (an ask id opened again on
     another or the same call after the short decision keeps its open card and bar). Test the
     reuse: ask A (i=5) open; short decision for A (ask-i=5) not held; a new ask with A's id opens
     (i=9) before the read returns; after the read, the i=9 card still has its bar and A's decided
     card shows the decision.
AC7. Session switch and close. A read that returns after the session was closed or switched
     (`closed`, or the view's subscription token changed, S23-fix7) changes nothing.
AC8. Paging. A short decision reached while paging backwards in one `behind` range whose ask entry
     is in an older page of the same range is drawn from the held entry, with no read (the whole
     range is reduced in index order once). Test it with the fake kernel's paging.
AC9. Fake. `fake/` gains a short-decision story step or fixture (both forms: held and not held,
     the latter with an ask entry stored as `part`s), for the tests and the user-sim.
AC10. Held cases of earlier adversaries, unchanged (assertions untouched; a wait may change only
